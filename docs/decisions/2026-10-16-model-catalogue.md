# Model catalogue

**Decided:** 2026-10-16

**Choice:** Designed on 2026-10-16 with [learned line models](2026-10-16-learned-line-models.md): `crates/core/src/models/catalogue.json`, changed only with a release. A model is listed only when the owner of its weights releases them under MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, CC0 or CC BY, its training data and their licences are documented, and plotline may host a converted copy. The first entry is `teed-1` (Soria et al., ICCV Workshops 2023); its training note says BIPED's terms allow only non-commercial use. `contour-1` (Chan, Durand and Isola, CVPR 2022) follows in its own change: its instance normalisation defeats tiling, so it will run on the whole image capped at 1024 pixels, converted from the authors' `model.zip`. Entries give the licence, the attribution and the training data, and say nothing about how drawings may be used. Built on 2026-10-16: `teed-1` is listed, at commit 6b7b215 of `plotline-models` (254,517 bytes, overlap 32). Changed on 2026-10-17 to commit bc9d7f9 (255,026 bytes, same overlap), whose graph has a fixed floor folded in so that blank paper reads 0: the first file drew a path on a flat white or grey picture at an ink threshold of 0.4, where nothing was. The core passes a model's answer through unchanged, so a catalogue model must put blank paper at 0 in its graph, and a core test pins that. The catalogue test also requires every overlap to be a multiple of 8 and under 256, because an overlap of 256 or more leaves no centre to keep and hangs the model stage, and 512 or more panics it. The card pictures under `web/public/models/` are drawn by `cargo run --release -p plotline-models --example model_cards -- web/public/models FILE...`, which takes model files named `<id>.onnx` that match the catalogue.

**Reason:** A user can read what a model is and where it came from before downloading it. The anime style of the same paper is left out because the source of its training sketches cannot be checked.
