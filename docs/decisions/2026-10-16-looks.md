# Looks

**Decided:** 2026-10-16

Amended on 2026-10-17, see [disc dots](2026-10-17-disc-dots.md): `grid` and `dither` were re-measured with filled dots, and Quick is offered only when its mean tone error is at most twice Balanced's, which leaves `dither` without one.

**Choice:** Built on 2026-10-16 under [the looks spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-looks-design.md), the owner's suggestion of presets within each style built from measured settings. A look is a named settings patch declared with the style in the registry, like its default overrides: `Style::with_look` and `Style::looks()`, listed in the style's schema. Every style has Balanced, its defaults, and may have Faithful, Quick and Fine, always under those plain names. A look's keys are the settings any look of the style sets; `Session::apply_look` sets them through `update`, so they count as the user's changes and a style switch carries them, and leaves every other setting, Pen use and the image adjustments included, alone. `Session::look` is the first look whose keys all match the settings, or none ("Custom"), worked out on each call, so Reset to defaults shows Balanced and Undo, presets and switches need nothing new. The GUI has a Look select under the style with a tour step and a browser flow, the CLI `--look NAME`, MCP `run(look=...)`, `get_session`'s `look` and the looks in `describe_style`, and Python `Session.apply_look` and `Session.look`. The values come from `cargo run --release -p plotline-core --example looks`, which scans each style's density setting from a quarter to four times the default, with `response` off and `auto`, on the gallery's three images at A4 with the 0.3 mm pen. No look may warn in the plot lint or plot longer than twice Balanced or an hour, whichever is more. Faithful is the lowest mean tone error, `auto` only where it is 5 % lower, and only where it beats Balanced by 10 %; Quick, where Balanced's median plot time is over 40 minutes, is the median of the budget fit for 30 minutes; Fine is the densest value whose tone error stays within a quarter of Balanced's, dropped within one step of Faithful. Measured on 2026-10-16 (mean tone error, Balanced then the look; minutes on the still life, the scene and the gradient): `grid` Quick 1.8 mm (0.151, 0.151; 7, 42, 30) and Fine 0.8 mm (0.149; 34, 204, 143); `hatch` Fine 0.7 mm (0.069, 0.077; 14, 50, 41); `waves` Faithful 2.8 mm (0.043, 0.021; 14, 26, 23) and Fine 1.0 mm (0.050; 23, 32, 30); `spiral` Fine 1.4 mm (0.026, 0.030; 18, 28, 26); `stipple` Fine 12,000 points (0.065, 0.065; 51, 52, 52); `line` Faithful `auto` and 20,000 points (0.149, 0.056; 9, 10, 9); `scribble` Faithful 20,000 points (0.089, 0.059; 24, 30, 35); `dashes` Faithful 12,000 points (0.075, 0.066; 49, 49, 48); `sketch` Faithful `auto` and 20,000 lines (0.301, 0.072; 20, 54, 51) and Fine 10,000 lines (0.136; 20, 20, 20); `tree` Faithful `auto` and 20,000 points (0.246, 0.088; 28, 29, 28); `mesh` Faithful `auto` and 6,000 points (0.112, 0.070; 21, 24, 21) and Fine 20,000 points (0.091; 49, 52, 50); `cells` Faithful `auto` and 8,400 points (0.100, 0.061; 42, 43, 42) and Fine 12,000 points (0.078; 57, 58, 58); `dither` Faithful 0.8 mm (0.048, 0.030; 64, 160, 145) and Quick 1.8 mm (0.077; 14, 34, 30); `circles` Faithful 1.5 mm (0.121, 0.046; 18, 48, 44); `flow` Faithful `auto` and 0.3 mm (0.177, 0.112; 14, 27, 32); `hilbert` Faithful `auto` and 0.3 mm (0.098, 0.081; 14, 32, 29); `mosaic` Faithful 0.3 mm (0.044, 0.017; 42, 60, 48); `truchet` Faithful 0.3 mm (0.126, 0.104; 12, 25, 23). `ink` has no density setting, `halftone` leaves specks on the gradient at its default and every denser spacing, and `contours` overdraws at its defaults, so they have Balanced alone. A law in `laws.rs` runs a look other than Balanced on one of the three images at A4 with the default pen and finds no lint warning or error. The gallery gets no look cells. The presets tour step and the README now say a preset keeps a setup This amends two rows of the brief's table: the Faithful look of `line`, `tree`, `mesh`, `cells`, `sketch`, `flow` and `hilbert` turns `tone.response` to `auto` ("Response defaults"), and the property laws gain `every_look_plots_clean`: a look other than Balanced, on one of the gallery's three images at A4 with the default pen, plots with no lint warning or error, 12 of its 72 cases a pull request ("Property laws").

**Reason:** Settings files hold a whole setup, the wrong size for a starting point inside one style, which should change the few settings that set its character and leave the image, page, pens and the user's own adjustments alone. A patch over the current settings does that, where a reset first would throw them away. Working the look out from the settings rather than storing a name keeps it right through every edit, undo and preset load. The first plan took Faithful as the median of Optimize's results per image and Fine as the densest clean value up to four times the default; a first measurement showed Optimize's eight trials land in different local minima of a bumpy error (`flow` with `auto`: 0.65, 1.05 and 1.29 mm), Faithful for `dither` going to the pen's width at ten times the plot time, and Fine for `waves` reaching 0.5 mm with twice the tone error. A scan scores each candidate on all three images at once, and the limits keep looks to drawings someone would plot. Gallery cells for looks would need a 0.3 mm column the grid lacks, for settings the registry diff already shows; the law and the example cover them.
