# Pen palette

**Decided:** 2026-10-16

**Choice:** Added on 2026-10-16 under [spec 13](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-pen-palette-design.md). For an image whose colourfulness passes 40, `analysis::measure` picks two to four of the ten `INKS` by forward stepwise selection (Hastie, Tibshirani and Friedman, *The Elements of Statistical Learning*, 2nd ed., 2009, §3.3.2): from bare paper it adds the ink that most lowers the mean CIE 1976 colour difference between the image and its reproduction under the Pen use colour model, and stops at four inks or when the next ink gains less than 2.3, about one just noticeable difference (Mahy, Van Eycken and Oosterlinck, 1994). The colours are 8 by 8 by 8 bins of the analysis grid, weighted by their pixels. `ImageStats` reports `palette` and `palette_error`. The `colour` suggestion's patch sets those inks as pens, lightest first at the first pen's width and named like "Yellow 0.3", and sets Pen use to by colour; the GUI's Apply, `plotline analyse` and MCP `analyse_image` with `set_pens` carry it

**Reason:** The colour note told users to add pens in the image's colours without saying which, and the inks are transparent, so the best set is not the image's commonest colours: green may be best drawn as yellow over blue. Fitting under the same model the Pen use stage solves picks the inks that stage will draw best with. Dominant colours by k-means or median cut matched to inks were left out for that reason. With the fit, `measure` takes about 34 ms natively on a 1024 by 768 colour scene (the median of seven runs of a release build on Windows), most of it the fit; trying every set of up to four would solve 385 sets against the greedy fit's 34, an estimated ten times as long, on every image in the browser. On the generated test images the fit picks yellow, green and blue for the landscape `synthetic::scene`, at a mean ΔE*ab of 3.4, and yellow, pink, purple and black for the colour bars, at 43, since no listed ink reaches their pure primaries. Only the ten listed inks are offered, since the bank's pens are all black and no ink is measured.

**Amends:** in [the brief's decision log](../BRIEF.md#14-decision-log), "Image analysis" (a patch may now hold `pens`), "Suggestion cut-offs" (the `colour` rule now needs a palette of two or more inks and carries a patch) and "Wasm size limit": the fit added about 28 KB to the WebAssembly module, which, built on Windows, was 1,907,309 bytes without the fit and 1,935,709 bytes with it.

**Amended on 2026-10-16:** see [palette from my pens](2026-10-16-palette-from-my-pens.md). `palette` lists `{name, color}` objects instead of ink names, and with a pen library the fit's candidates are the library's colours instead of the ten inks.
