# Optimize scans and keeps the looks' limits

**Decided:** 2026-10-16. Amends the brief's "Optimize" row.

**Choice:** Optimize tries the current value first, then a grid in half-octave steps from a quarter to four times it within the schema's range, at most nine runs. A value that the grid gives twice after rounding runs once. The current value is used as set, without rounding, so the first trial's drawing is the drawing of the current settings. A trial does not count when its drawing plots for more than twice the current value's time, or an hour if that is more, or when it has a plot lint warning or error that the current value's drawing does not have. A budget in minutes replaces the time limit. The suggestion states its plot time and the current value's time. A trial whose run fails is left out with its message. Each trial records `minutes`, `slow`, `warnings` and `failed`. The law `an_optimize_suggestion_keeps_the_limits` checks that an applied suggestion plots in the time it reports, within the limit, and with no new warning.

**Reason:** The tone error has more than one dip across the density range. The looks measurement found Optimize's eight golden-section trials landing in different dips on different images (`flow` with `auto`: 0.65, 1.05 and 1.29 mm, see [the looks decision](2026-10-16-looks.md)). On a small still life, `hatch` scored 0.035 at 1 mm, 0.041 at 1.414 mm and 0.034 at 2 mm. With no budget, the search also pushed `dither` down to the pen's width, at ten times the plot time. A scan of the same range costs one more run and cannot settle in the wrong dip between its grid points. The limits are the ones a look must keep, measured against the user's current value rather than the defaults. The lint rule only counts new warnings, because a user's own setup may already warn, and then no value would pass. A minutes budget is the user's own time limit, so it replaces ours. A trial whose run fails, such as a `halftone` value past the dot vertex budget, is left out with its message instead of ending the search; only the first trial's failure ends it, since that is the current settings' own run. The scan always reaches four times denser, and without this rule `halftone` on the scene and the gradient at A4 failed the whole search at 0.5 mm.

Measured natively on the three test images at A4 with the defaults and no budget: the limits held `grid` and `dither` at 0.707 mm, about twice the plot time, where the lowest tone error lay at 0.25 to 0.354 mm and 300 to 2,000 minutes; `circles` at 1.061 to 1.5 mm and 34 to 48 minutes, against 86 to 94 minutes unlimited; and `truchet` at its current 0.4 mm, every denser value warning or too slow. `hatch` (1.4 to 2.8 mm), `waves` (2.8 mm) and `flow` went sparser or barely slower, as before. A search took 1.3 to 5 s for the line and dot styles, and 16 to 33 s for the Voronoi styles, whose count reruns the relaxation. Each trial runs the plot lint's measure, about 150 ms for `hatch` on A4 against about 40 ms for the cached run itself, so `hatch` takes 1.3 s where the brief recorded 0.3 to 2.2 s across styles. Leaving out only the SSIM saved nothing measurable, since the counts the lint needs are the cost, so the measure is the session's own.

**Amended on 2026-10-16: the quickest of the near-best.** The best trial is now the quickest to plot of those within the limits whose tone error is within a tenth of the lowest, the earliest of equal times, instead of the trial with the lowest error. The result also gives the time limit in minutes (`limit_minutes`, none when a budget in minutes replaced it) and, as `beyond`, the trial left out only for its plot time with the lowest tone error, when that error is more than a tenth under every trial's within the limits. The sentence says "best for its plot time", and when there is such a trial it names it with its tone error and time and the limit it passed: the GUI says to type a target in minutes to allow it, the CLI and MCP that a budget in minutes replaces the limit. The law `an_optimize_suggestion_keeps_the_limits` also checks that no trial within the limits and within a tenth of the lowest error plots quicker than the suggestion, and that `beyond` is a slow trial more than a tenth under the lowest. A browser flow sets a 40 s pen lift, draws the sample at `hatch` with an 8 mm spacing and checks that Optimize names a closer spacing over the limit.

**Reason:** A user's line-art drawing (a pale octopus with fine texture) at `hatch` with a 0.2 mm pen got "already best" at 0.8 mm and a denser spacing from every sparser start, so Optimize seemed to say only "denser". Measured natively, the error falls steadily from 0.056 at 3.3 mm to about 0.019 at 0.8 mm and is flat below, from 0.016 to 0.022 between 0.5 and 0.8 mm, with neighbouring spacings up to a fifth apart: 0.016 at 0.58 mm, 0.018 at 0.59 mm, 0.020 at 0.60 mm. Shifting the drawing by a fraction of a raster cell moves the error by only 1 to 6 %, so the spread is the marks falling differently on the image's detail, plus hatch lines at exactly 45° locking to the 0.1 mm raster: at 44.7° the spread between 0.53 and 0.61 mm halves, from 8 % to 3.6 %. Picking the lowest error picked these accidents with plot time: from 1.65 mm it chose 0.583 mm at 47 min over 0.825 mm at 30 min. On the gallery's three images at A4 with the defaults it bought tiny gains the same way: `grid` on the scene 0.707 mm at 0.148 and 259 min against 2 mm at 0.152 and 35 min, which the new rule picks; `grid` on the still life 0.707 mm at 43 min against 2.828 mm at 3 min for 2 % more error; `dither` on the scene 0.707 mm at 203 min against its current 1 mm at 103 min for 7 %; `dashes` on the still life 14,142 points at 57 min against 7,071 at 29 min for 2 %. A tenth is the margin a look must beat Balanced by ([the looks decision](2026-10-16-looks.md)); a fifth started giving up gains larger than the spread, such as `line` on the scene at 0.098 instead of 0.088 and `scribble` on the scene at 0.059 instead of 0.053. The styles whose error falls steeply, such as `waves`, `circles`, `mosaic` and `sketch`, pick as before. A tenth does not cover the whole spread at 45°, so on the octopus from 1.65 mm it still picks 0.583 mm; a raster that does not lock to 45° lines is the open follow-up. The note on `beyond` answers why the search stopped where it did: from 3.3 mm, at the user's speeds (about a second a pen lift), it suggests 1.65 mm at 43 min and says that 0.825 mm follows the tones at 0.020 against 0.038 but takes 123 min, over the hour, so the user can type a target to allow it.

**Amended on 2026-10-17: a search with no score says why.** When no trial has a tone error within the limits, `OptimizeRun::result` no longer returns `None` for a search that ended done. It returns the current value with `error` null, no patch, no `beyond`, and a `reason` that says why: every drawing is empty ("No value of spacing_mm from 0.25 to 4 mm draws anything here", with the empty-drawing advice of the plot lint), no drawing follows the image's tones enough for a tone error (the tone match's gain stays under its minimum), or the limits left every trial out, counted by cause. A trial with no marks is told apart by its plot time of zero. The GUI writes the same sentence under the setting's label, the CLI prints it with no `--set`, and MCP `optimize` returns it instead of `null`. The law `an_optimize_suggestion_keeps_the_limits` checks that a search that ends done has a result, and that a result with no tone error has no patch and no trial within the limits with a tone error.

**Reason:** The audit of 2026-10-16 ([F10](../audits/2026-10-16-colour-styles-audit.md#f10-optimize-can-finish-with-no-result-and-the-gui-then-says-nothing-confirmed)) found searches ending with no result on empty drawings (`grid` or `hatch` on a pale picture) and on `contours` for 7 of 20 pictures, whose outlines follow the tone too little for a tone error. The GUI then showed nothing, the CLI said "no trial stayed within the target" with no target given, MCP returned a bare `null`, and the audit's own harness assumed a result and failed. A result that is always there, with the reason in it, gives every caller the answer in the one place it already reads. Refusing to start on an empty drawing, which the audit also offered, was left out: a denser value can draw where the current one does not, so the search still has something to try.
