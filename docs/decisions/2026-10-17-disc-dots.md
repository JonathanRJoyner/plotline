# Disc dots for grid and dither

**Decided:** 2026-10-17

**Choice:** `grid` and `dither` draw each dot as a filled disc whose width is a share of the grid spacing, instead of one touch of the pen. Finding F1 of the [colour and styles audit](../audits/2026-10-16-colour-styles-audit.md) recommended it.

- The two styles keep their `render` stage, the dot renderer, so their stage lists and existing presets stay valid. The renderer gains one setting, `render.size`: the dot's width as a share of the spacing, from 0.1 to 1.5, default 1, where neighbouring dots touch. Each dot is drawn the way the stipple renderer draws one (`stipple::dot_run`): a spiral out to the rim, or one vertex when the dot is no wider than the pen, under the same 20-million-vertex budget. There is no `vary`, since error diffusion already puts the tone in where the dots go and the grid's threshold makes it one level.
- The placers hand the renderer their spacing: `Points` carries the pitch of the grid its points lie on (`Points.1`), which the grid and error-diffusion placers set and the others leave empty. The dot renderer refuses points that lie on no grid.
- The plot lint follows the share. The `dark` finding's fix grows `render.size` towards the 50 % target when that widens the dots, which costs no pen lifts; a closer spacing would shrink the dots with it, so it is offered only while they are no wider than the pen. The `overdraw` fix brings a share over 1 back to 1, and offers nothing at or under 1, where a spacing change cannot separate dots that grow with it.
- The size setting is marked `"x-share-of": "spacing"` in its schema, and a law in `crates/core/tests/laws.rs`, `dots_sized_by_their_grid_ink_their_share_of_a_dark_page_at_any_spacing`, draws every style with such a setting on a page of full ink at a random spacing, share and pen: the grid's cells must be inked at least 85 % of `PI / 4 * (dot / spacing)^2`, the dot being the share of the spacing or the pen where that is wider, capped at the `PI / 4` of touching dots. The margin is the dot geometry's: arcs are sampled at most a quarter pen apart ("Dot geometry"), so a rim under `pen / (4 PI)` from the centre is drawn as a short stroke across the dot, which inks 89.5 % of it at worst; a 4x run on seed 1 found a 0.54 mm pen in a 0.61 mm dot inking 91 % on a 0.02 mm raster. The law passed at 10 times its cases on nine seeds.
- The budget fit law, `a_fit_lands_near_its_target_or_says_how_near_it_can`, no longer asks a fit to pen-down metres that falls short to be as near as the end of the range when the style's density setting drives its points or lines rather than its length, as the density law already judges those styles. A disc's spiral is about its area over the pen width long, so these drawings' length stays within about 2 % at any spacing (14.45 to 14.82 m in the case that broke it), and which spacing comes nearest is noise.
- The golden files for `grid` set `render.size` to 0.1, so each dot stays the one vertex the Python engine drew and the files still pin where the grid puts its points.
- The looks were measured again with `cargo run --release -p plotline-core --example looks -- grid dither` (mean tone error, Balanced then the look; minutes on the still life, the scene and the gradient). `grid`: Balanced (0.146; 29, 174, 122), Quick 4.4 mm (0.157; 7, 44, 31) and Fine 0.7 mm (0.146; 51, 307, 215). `dither`: Balanced (0.025; 55, 137, 124) and Faithful 0.8 mm (0.016; 77, 193, 175). The looks example now offers Quick only when its mean tone error is at most twice Balanced's. Dither's budget fit for 30 minutes found 4.9 mm (0.238; 13, 32, 30), nine times Balanced's error: the dots' spirals alone take most of half an hour, so only a spacing that loses the picture gets there. Only `grid` and `dither` had a Quick look, so the rule changes no other style. The [looks decision](2026-10-16-looks.md) is amended to say so.
- `halftone` stays its own style. It is the grid placer with the stipple renderer, which sizes dots in mm and by the ink; `grid` is the one-level poster and the engine's first style, so folding it into a halftone preset would rename what the CLI, MCP and presets start on for no gain in the drawing.

This amends the brief's rows "Dot geometry" (grid and dither now draw their dots with it), "Grid spacing and empty stages" (the dot renderer now has a setting, and the golden files set it to 0.1) and "Cross-engine check" (likewise).

**Reason:** A dot was one pen touch, so with the default 1 mm spacing and 0.3 mm pen a cell at full ink was 7 % covered, and the audit measured drawings that reached only 6 to 9 % ink at black over 20 varied pictures. A closer grid cannot fix that at a sane plot time, because every dot is a pen lift. Drawing the dot to fill its cell changes the dot count not at all.

The share of the spacing, rather than a size in mm, keeps the coverage when the spacing moves: in the GUI, in Optimize and the budget fit, which move only the density setting, and in the looks. With a size in mm, a closer spacing would pile the dots on each other and a wider one would leave them small in their cells, so Optimize would have scored coverage rather than detail. With the share, a disc's spiral is about its area over the pen width long, so the pen-down length stays about the same at any spacing and the spacing trades detail against pen lifts alone.

The default share was measured with `crates/core/examples/probe.rs` on four of the audit's pictures, A4, one 0.3 mm black pen (tone error, SSIM, reach at black, minutes):

At 1, dither's tone error was the lowest or within 0.002 of it on every picture and its SSIM within 0.02 of the best, for 5 % more plot time than at 0.9. Wider than 1, the discs overlap where the ink is dense, which lifts the floor and gains little. At 1 the dots still read as dots: touching discs leave the corners between them white. Grid's tone error barely moves with the share, since its threshold already makes it one level, and its SSIM rises with the share like dither's.

The style picker's pictures need no change for these styles. They draw every mark at least 2 px wide ([dots at small sizes](2026-10-16-dots-at-small-sizes.md)), which made one-touch dots look five times their area (the audit's F9); a 1 mm disc at the picture's 600 px is about 3 px wide, so the floor no longer enlarges it, and the cell now shows what the style plots. The floor stays for the line styles, whose 0.3 mm lines are under a pixel there.
