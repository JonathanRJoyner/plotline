Section: Styles

- **Looks you can trust:** the looks were measured again on nine pictures, by tone and contrast, and every one was looked at. No look now draws its marks more than twice as sparsely as the defaults, so Grid's Quick, which turned the still life into a few large dots, is gone. Several looks changed, Spiral's Quick and Flow's Faithful were dropped by eye, and Waves and Hilbert gained a Quick.
- **Cells stay off white paper:** Cells has a new setting, `cells.max_edge_mm` (16 mm by default), that leaves out the walls between points farther apart than that, so the cells at the edge of an inked area no longer reach across the white paper to the page edge.
