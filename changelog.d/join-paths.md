- **Joined paths:** Path order has a new setting, `join`. `on` joins the paths that meet into the fewest pen-down trails and draws an edge two paths share once; `retrace` also draws some short edges twice, so the pen lifts much less. `cells`, `mesh` and `tree` now default to `retrace`, which cuts their pen lifts by 46 to 86 % and their plot time by 11 to 48 % in the regression gallery, for 4 to 19 % more pen-down length laid over lines already drawn; `cells` no longer gets the lift-heavy lint finding. `contours` defaults to `on`, which draws its repeated stretches of line once. Presets of those four styles saved earlier draw differently; set `order.join` to `off` for the old drawing.
