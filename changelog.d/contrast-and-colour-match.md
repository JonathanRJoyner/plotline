- **Contrast and colour in the tone match:** the stats, the CLI line, the MCP result and the GUI now show the drawing's contrast, how much of the image's range from light to dark it reaches, beside the tone error and SSIM. A drawing in more than one colour also shows its colour difference from the image in CIELAB (ΔE), beside blank paper's. The tone error never reads above blank paper's, so `contours` no longer shows errors of 2 to 12. Optimize now prefers the darker of two drawings that follow the tones alike: on the gallery's pictures its picks reach a median 1.4 times the contrast, where before it drifted to faint grids and sparse dots.
