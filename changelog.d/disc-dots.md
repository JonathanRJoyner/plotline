Section: Styles

- **Filled dots for `grid` and `dither`:** each dot is now a filled disc as wide as its grid cell, drawn with a spiral like the stipple dots, instead of one touch of the pen. The new `render.size` setting is the dot's width as a share of the spacing (1, neighbours touching, by default), so a closer spacing gives smaller dots rather than a lighter page. Dark areas went from about 7 % ink to about 78 % with the 0.3 mm pen, so the two styles no longer look blank, and dither's tone error on a portrait fell from 0.057 to 0.020, for about a third more plot time at the same dot count. A share that makes the dot no wider than the pen, 0.3 at the default spacing and pen, gives the old one-touch dots. The `dark` lint finding no longer fires at the defaults, and for these styles its fix now grows the dots instead of crowding them. The looks were measured again: `grid` Quick is 5.6 mm and Fine 0.7 mm, and `dither` keeps Faithful at 0.8 mm but has no Quick, since a 30-minute dither lost the picture.
